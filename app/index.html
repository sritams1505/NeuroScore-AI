Under the Hood

About the Prediction Model

🌲

Extra Trees Regressor

An ensemble of extremely randomized decision trees is used to regress the mental health wellness score from behavioral and demographic features.

📊

12 Input Signals

Age, gender, country, academic level, platform usage, purpose of use, daily usage hours, unlock frequency, study hours, physical activity, sleep, and stress level all feed the model.

⚡

Real-Time Inference

Requests are served synchronously via a FastAPI backend with Pydantic-validated payloads, returning a score on a continuous 0–10 wellness scale.

🔒

Not a Diagnosis

This tool produces a statistical estimate for research and awareness purposes only. It is not a clinical or diagnostic instrument.
